ReefLog

Aquarium water-test logbook for iPhone

Log alkalinity, calcium, magnesium, nitrate, phosphate, salinity and more. See trends against your target ranges, get forecasts and dosing help, and never miss a test day.

Support

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel ReefLog Pro in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open ReefLog › Settings › Restore purchases.
