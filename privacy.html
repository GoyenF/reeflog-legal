ReefLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

ReefLog keeps your aquarium data on your iPhone. The developer never receives it.

What ReefLog stores

Your tanks, test results, target ranges and settings are stored on your device only. ReefLog has no accounts and no servers of its own.

What ReefLog collects

Nothing. ReefLog has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and sends no information about you to the developer or to third parties.

Reminders

If you turn on test reminders, ReefLog schedules local notifications on your device. They are not sent through any server.

CSV export

When you export your data, ReefLog creates a file on your device and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. ReefLog only receives confirmation of which features you've unlocked.

Deleting your data

Delete a tank in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
